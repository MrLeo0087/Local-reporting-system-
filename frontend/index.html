How It Works

From a problem on your street to a resolved report — three steps, fully tracked.

1

Report

Snap a photo, pick the category and your ward, and optionally drop a pin on the map.

2

Route & Review

Your report goes straight to the department responsible for that category and ward — no manual sorting.

3

Track to Resolution

Watch the status change live — verified, in progress, completed — with a full history you can check anytime.
